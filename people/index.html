Students & collaborators

Research in this area is collaborative by nature — reconstructions, models and datasets are built with students and long-term co-authors across several institutions, and much of it is developed together with the EarthByte Group and the GPlates project.

Groups & software

Two long-running collaborations that most of this work sits inside.

🌐 EarthByte Group

A research group at the University of Sydney building open datasets and models of Earth's plate tectonic, geodynamic and paleogeographic evolution. I was a Research Fellow with EarthByte from 2010–2019 and remain a close collaborator.

earthbyte.org →

🗺️ GPlates

Open-source software for building and visualising plate tectonic reconstructions, developed by EarthByte and collaborators including the University of Sydney, Caltech and Norway's GPlates.org team. I contribute to GPlates and its Python ecosystem — see Plate tectonic reconstructions.

gplates.org →
